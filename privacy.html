Data Protection Protocols & Infrastructure Isolation Standards

Effective Date: May 2026 | Policy Scope: Global Enterprise Deployments

ReadyHand Artificial Intelligence operates as an enterprise software development entity specialized in building secure administrative frameworks, learning management structures, and high-precision automation engines. This policy transparently governs the processes through which we safeguard information handled within our web architectures, customer portals, custom mobile applications, and biometric integration sync nodes.

1. Information Categorization

Our architectures process telemetry and operational records into three structural divisions depending on your deployed configuration:

Data Layer Collected Components Processing Intent
Institutional Base Data Names, business emails, phone numbers, structural fee metrics, and student/personnel configurations. Powering Student Management Systems and corporate enterprise database configurations.
Biometric Logs Hardware terminal logs, device identification hashes, and timestamp strings. Synchronizing employee/student tracking timelines into automated HRMS reporting pipelines.

2. Information Roles: Controllers vs. Processors

Understanding your organization's legal position is vital to operational compliance:

  • The Client as Controller: Your institution controls employee, client, parent, and student data collections. You remain strictly responsible for collecting authentic permissions from end-users before onboarding them to biometric or portal architectures.
  • ReadyHand Artificial Intelligence as Processor: We act purely under your administrative directions. We maintain database tables, manage server security profiles, and build isolated channels to securely transport data.

3. Data Retention & Infrastructure Isolation

We execute server-side data retention parameters built to align with institutional agreements:

  • Isolation: Each company or institution runs on structurally isolated database schemas. This completely prevents cross-tenant leaks or unauthorized information leakage.
  • Biometric Privacy: Our systems do not store raw biological fingerprint images or facial photographs on cloud servers. Hardware logs are securely converted into mathematical code hashes locally on the physical sensor layer before being securely synced to the cloud.
  • Lifespan: Records persist on active host nodes for the absolute duration of your active subscription framework. Decommissioned data sets are structurally purged using cryptographic deletion models.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Transfers

ReadyHand Artificial Intelligence does not trade, distribute, or leak your institutional database elements to marketing companies or third-party ad networks. Information passes exclusively through secure tunnels to pre-approved cloud platforms necessary to execute your workflows:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API engines for operational transaction dispatches.
  • Encrypted banking channels and authorized payment gateways.
  • Enterprise cloud storage hosting clusters (such as AWS and Google Cloud Platform).

5. End-User Rights & Data Access

System end-users (such as parents, students, or workforce members) who wish to inspect, adjust, or completely remove information records from their profile logs must submit requests directly to their institutional administrator. Because our engineers cannot manipulate individual records without an institutional security token, your administrative desk remains the primary controller for user validation requests.
