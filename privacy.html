1. About Institute CoreX

Institute CoreX is a cloud-based educational and institutional management platform developed, owned, and operated by RHAI TECHNOLOGY.

The platform is designed to serve multiple independent educational institutes, academies, coaching centers, schools, and training organizations through a scalable Software-as-a-Service model.

Features may include student management, teacher management, attendance, homework, assignments, fee management, communication, academic tracking, reports, biometric integrations, and other institutional management services.

The educational institution, such as Arise Academy, is generally responsible for collecting and managing its institutional, student, parent, teacher, and staff information.

6. Multi-Institute Data Separation

Institute CoreX is designed as a multi-institute platform. Access to institutional information is managed through authentication, roles, permissions, and data access controls.

Authorized users of one client institution are not intended to access the private records of another independent institution without appropriate authorization.

Certain client institutes may choose to integrate biometric or face-recognition systems with Institute CoreX for attendance or identity verification.

The client institution is responsible for obtaining any necessary consent, authorization, or legal permission required for the use of biometric or identity-verification technologies.

Institute CoreX may process attendance verification results, timestamps, device logs, and related technical information required for system synchronization.
